Who are we and what are we trying to do?

A community first,
a corp second

Rift Watch Gaming is a casual community that's active in and out of EVE Online. In EVE we fly as part of the wider Watchpoint group, focusing on combat, support and shared experiences.

We're friendly and social, with a high sec base, small gang PvP and plenty of teamwork. Pilots of every skill level are welcome, and our Discord is where it all comes together.

Friendship First

A community in game and out. It's about enjoying EVE with people you genuinely like flying with.

Small Fleet Specialists

Small gang fleets where you can learn and practise every role. Great for new pilots and veterans alike.

Faction Warfare

Mercenary roams for Gallente, Caldari or whoever needs us. We'll help new players get set up and their standings above zero.

NPSI and Beyond

We fly with NPSI communities for extra content: small gang, bigger fights, PvE and ISK making.

Learn and Grow

No elitism. We learn by doing, with mentors on hand to help you find your feet.

Events and Play Times

EU prime time fleets on Monday, Thursday and Sunday, roughly 18:00 to 22:30 EVE time, with casual play on other evenings.

About us

and up for anything

RWG in general

A small community of casual players who enjoy games, anime, movie nights, D&D and just hanging out together on Discord.

Our EVE community

Formed in 2022, with around 7 to 10 pilots on most days plus friends, occasional flyers and alts. We range from our 20s to our 70s, mostly in Europe, with some in South Africa and a couple on US time. We're more of a close group of friends than a traditional corp.

What we do in game

Small gang PvP and Faction Warfare, plus fleet planning, fit testing and mentoring. We also dabble in escalations, wormholes, Abyssals and NPSI fleets, usually in a relaxed way on Discord.

Who we're looking for

Active, enthusiastic, social and relaxed pilots who'd rather be part of a smaller group than a number in a big one.

FAQ

Questions we get asked

When do you play?

Corp fleets run Monday, Thursday and Sunday. On other evenings there's spontaneous content and plenty going on in Discord. Weekends are usually quieter.

What are your short and long term goals?

Short term, recruit like minded pilots. Long term, build our skills in small gang PvP, Faction Warfare and group content while keeping the social feel we've got now.

Do I need to join Discord?

We strongly encourage it. Discord is the heart of the community, and members who didn't get involved there have struggled to settle in. Lurking is fine to start with.

We have a strict no toxicity policy.

Why are you recruiting so few?

Quality over quantity. We want to keep the close knit feel, and we may open up a wider intake later.

What are you looking for?

Social, active, friendly pilots who are happy on Discord, take a bit of initiative and have a "let's give it a shot" attitude. Mistakes are fine. That's how we all learn.
